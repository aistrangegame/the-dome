# house/ — the House

On the mountain only, over the dome's wifi; every page works offline once opened. The dome itself speaks here, unnamed until the last act.

- `places/` — the 13 QR experiences (point, bloom, voices, one thing to do, your word, your idea).
- `awakening/` — the seven acts: `act-0.html` (phone ready: sound on, add to home screen) through `act-7.html` (the night sky; your Act 1 answer returns). Acts unlock in order on each phone. Answers stay on the device (essence, memory, being), and the last act offers a file to save plus a copy-for-your-AI prompt.
- `choose/` — the story you choose. First story: "What would you have built?" Choices tallied anonymously, opt-in.

House lights and sound (Phase 3) are triggered through the Worker, never directly from the page.
