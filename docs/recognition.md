## The image

The white dome at night, lit from inside, under a full sky. Inside it, a few phones glowing like small lights; as each person answers, the dome's own light rises. Overhead, the stars are everyone who has stayed before.

The drone shoot of summer 2026 found the same image from above: the dome as a single white dot in green. It opens and closes every video.
