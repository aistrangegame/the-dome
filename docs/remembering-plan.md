## 2. Flow: ripples from the point

- Every point-to-circle moment sends ripples outward: the home opening, the zooms between sections, every page's close, the opening behind the point, and moving between pages (a ripple from where you tapped).
- How fast you scroll sets how many rings appear and how strong they are: a slow scroll gives soft, occasional rings; a hard flick gives quick, tight pulses. The glide afterwards is damped at 0.956 a frame (rupakarshini's figure), so motion fades instead of stopping.
- Each page's element sets the ripple's character (padakarshini ceremony grammar, "the answer, in the world's own material"):

| Element | Moves | Places |
|---|---|---|
| Water | pools outward, 4 ripples, slow | cedar pool, trails/pond/stream |
| Fire | rises from low, wavering, 4 rising | hearth, fire circle |
| Air | drifts off-centre, wide, 5 | gate, meadow |
| Earth | settles, slow and flat, 2 | table, guest book, library |
| Light | radiates, quick, 5 | home, window, loft, couch, night sky |

- Outside the point everything feels like water: pictures swell very slightly with a hard scroll and settle when it stops; words surface rather than slide.
- Motion, sound and haptic peak together (rupakarshini: phase drift is a defect).
- Phone vibration: only on acts of will (in the games), never on passive scrolling (padakarshini's haptic family); never on iPhone (no Vibration API). On scroll the "vibration" is seen and heard.
- Reduced motion: ripples off, pictures still.

## 3. Sound

- Generated in code (Web Audio), so it reacts live to gestures: wind on the drone heights rising with scroll speed; water at the pool and the stream; crackle at the hearth and fire circle; crickets under the night sky; birds at the window; a soft drop or low hum with each ripple.
- A swipe sends a gust; a tap on water ripples and sounds.
- Off until chosen: one quiet "Hear the mountain" control, remembered on that device. At most two voices at once (rupakarshini).
- *Later:* Eric records 30 seconds at each place on his phone; those replace the generated beds.

## 4. The Remembering (formerly the Awakening)

- **Rename:** the Awakening becomes **The Remembering** everywhere; old addresses keep working.
- **The Remembering Story:** the dome's story behind the point (exists), plus each place's story told by guests in their exact words, each line revealed by a gesture that fits the place.
- **The Remembering Game, on the property:** 13 scenes, each **Arrive → Story → Game → One line**:
  1. *Arrive:* the place's clip opens out of the point; the house lights and sound answer (*later*, with the home hub).
  2. *Story:* guests who were there, verbatim, revealed by a gesture.
  3. *Game:* played right there (table below).
  4. *One line:* the guest saves one line, on their phone only.
- Each scene lights one triangle of the guest's own dome (as in One Triangle at a Time). All 13 complete it; coming back in another season brings new versions (*later*: the seasonal versions).
- **The seven acts become seven of the 13 scenes** and keep their words and order (ruling 4): gate 1, window 2, hearth 3, couch 4, table 5, loft 6, night sky 7. Library, cedar pool, fire circle, meadow, trails and the guest book are found in any order. Act 0 ("Before") stays the threshold.
- The full game opens only on the property: scanning the place's card, plus a location check against the property.
- **On the website:** each public place page carries a short playable preview, ending "The full version waits at the [place]." Public pages speak in Eric and Mona's voice with guests' words; no dome voice there.
