## How Eric walks it

1. He opens **dome.aistrangegame.com/guide**: a guidebook (the four layers, a six-step route, his tools, what's real and what isn't yet), then his code.
2. Signed in for 30 days on that device. Every page now carries **＋ Add a note** and **My notes** (bottom right).
3. **Add a note:** tap any part of the page (or Tab to it and press Enter), or the whole page; type or speak. Speech is written out as he talks where the browser can; the recording goes with it.
4. **Own words:** each dashed box of his has **Say it in your own words**. It saves a draft; **It sounds like me. Use it.** replaces the stand-in. Approved words can be changed again later.
5. **Move the pins:** on the places map, drag a pin (or use the arrow keys on a focused one) and save.
6. **My notes:** everything he left, its status and our reply, signed *Claude · Ashrey Intelligence*.

Every note, own-words draft and pin fix is a row in **Eric's Notes** (Airtable, one table, `Kind` = Note / Own words / Fix). To answer: write the `Reply`, set `Status` (Heard / Changing / Changed / Answered). Never delete a row; set it to OLD.
