# Handoff, October 14, 2026: ready to show Eric and Mona

The showcase is complete enough to walk with them. It runs in the browser with no server: everything they do in it stays on their own device until the Worker goes live.

## How to walk them through it

1. Open the showcase and add `?notes=1` to the address (or press **Notes** in the bottom-left corner). Every page then shows numbered notes before each part, and **This page's guide** opens a panel: what the page is for, who it's for, where its words come from, what it connects to, and what changes when it goes live. The **whole house** tab lists every page in its four layers.
2. Suggested order: Home → Places (touch the dome on the map) → one place page → Stories → Timeline → Ideas → Stay (book two test nights) → Your stays → the dashboard (from the footer column that appears with notes on) → the House (a place on the mountain, the Awakening, What would you have built?) → the point in the top-left corner (the dome's story and its three games).
3. With notes on, the dashed boxes are where their own words go. Each holds a stand-in and the questions to answer. "Hide where your words go" removes the boxes to show the page without them.
