# Media plan

## Folders

- `media/` holds the **originals** exactly as uploaded. Never edited, renamed or recompressed. File names match the `File name` field in the Airtable Media table.
- `site/assets/media/` holds **web copies**, made by `scripts/make-web-media.sh`:
  - photos: `{name}-1600.webp`, `{name}-800.webp`, `{name}-400.webp`
  - video: `{name}-1080.mp4` and `{name}-720.mp4` (H.264, `+faststart`, no audio unless chosen), plus `{name}-poster.webp`
  - clips: cut to 6 to 10 seconds with in/out points from the Media row's *Clip moments*

## How the motion is used

- **Opening and closing.** The top-down clip that rises in a circle until the dome is a white dot on green, with a ring turning the opposite way around it. It opens every video and, reversed, closes it.
- **The page that breathes.** Green ground with the dome as a small white dot behind the sections; the dot drifts as the reader scrolls.
- **Transitions and parallax.** The angle clips between sections and as slow backgrounds.
