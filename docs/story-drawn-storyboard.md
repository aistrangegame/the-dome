# The Dome's Own Story, drawn: storyboard

*Oct 28, 2026. For Ashrey's ruling before anything is drawn. Source: `site/play/story.html` (nine stages, the dome's voice, behind the point). Engine lineage: `site/timeline-motion.html` + `assets/js/timeline-art/kit.js`. Ships as B beside the scroll version (A), the way the timeline did.*

## The spine

The timeline engine has one rule at its heart: when you move on, the moment **folds back into the point**. Nothing stays. That is right for a timeline: each moment is one guest's memory, borrowed and returned.

It is wrong for this story. The dome's whole claim is the opposite: *nothing given here is lost; it comes back.* "I held every bit of it, and kept none of it" is the dome being modest. The drawing should not be modest.

So the drawn story is **one drawing, never erased.** One canvas. The point draws the meadow in chapter I and that meadow is still there in chapter IX. The struts raised in II are the same struts the guests light in V, go unseen in VII, and lift into the sky at the end. Moving on does not fold anything; the camera moves (transform only, as ruled) and the point keeps drawing onto what is already there. By the last chapter the reader is looking at everything the dome has ever been, at once.

Nine palettes, one line that never breaks.

## Three versions, briefly

**V1 (the safe one):** nine drawings to the `kit.js` contract exactly as the three moments were made. Gold line on dark green, a hero per chapter, fold between them. Competent. It would look like the timeline with the words changed.

**V2 (what I thought was possible):** a colour world per chapter through the `css` hook; the real drone film inside circles at the beginning and the end; the dome's own light rising chapter by chapter (the Remembering already carries that ladder, 0.02 → 0.62); 252 triangles lit by season in chapter V using the data *One Triangle at a Time* already has; the three timeline moments reused as miniatures in VI. Still nine separate drawings, still folding.

**V3 (the one to build):** V2's colour and material, with the fold removed and the nine drawings made one. The point is not a narrator's cursor; it is the dome's own awareness, and it is born on the reader's first push. The lattice is not a backdrop; it is the protagonist, built once and never redrawn. The guests are not quotes beside a picture; they are the triangles the dome is made of. And the "inner sky and outer sky" is finally drawn: in IX the lit triangles leave the dome and become the stars.

## What the build needs (for the Sonnet session)

1. **One canvas.** One SVG holding every chapter's group; the camera is a transform on a wrapper (viewBox never changes mid-motion; zooms by transform only, as ruled). `TL.chapter(n, def)` instead of `TL.register(title, def)`: a chapter's `strokes`, `hero`, `swell`, `fills` draw onto the shared canvas, and nothing is reset on moving on. Reduced motion: each chapter's end state, cut to cut.
2. **The lattice as data.** A real geodesic projection (2V or 3V frequency, ~250 to 300 visible triangles), built once in II, each triangle an element with an index. Chapter V assigns guests to triangles in the order they came, coloured by `seasonColor`, from `reviews.js`; IX lifts the same elements to star positions by a second transform.
3. **Palettes per chapter** through CSS custom properties on `.ch-{n}` (the existing `css` hook), with chapter III the only light ground.
4. **Films in circles** (exists): `topdown` in IX; optionally the turning clip in I.
5. **Performance:** the drop-shadow filter only on the chapter currently drawing; the 252 triangles are plain fills; test at 390 on a real iPhone before it goes anywhere.
6. **Sound:** beds from `flow.js` as listed; chapter tones on the Remembering's ladder (174 → 528).
7. **Address:** `site/play/story-drawn.html` as B; the story room's opening offers A and B; if Ashrey chooses B it becomes `story.html` and A stays linked.
