How to walk it

Under 33 minutes, on your phone or computer.

Leave a note whenever something strikes you.

  1. Home. Scroll slowly from the top: the camera comes down out of the sky onto the dome.
  2. The places. Touch the dome on the map to see the seven places inside. Open one or two, and play the game on each: every place has its own.
  3. Stories and the timeline. Told only in guests’ own words. The timeline comes two ways (see the one choice below).
  4. Stay. Book two pretend nights. Nothing is charged.
  5. On the mountain. Open Menu (top right) and choose On the mountain. Play a place’s game as a guest standing there would. It ends with one line to keep and lights one triangle of their dome.
  6. Your dashboard. In the Menu, under For Eric and Mona. Set a night’s price, then look at the Stay page: it reads the same prices.
  7. The point. The white dot in the top-left corner.

What’s real, and what isn’t yet

So nothing surprises you.

  • Every guest quote is word for word, with their first name.
  • The dashed boxes hold our stand-ins until your words arrive. Nothing in them is yours until you say it.
  • Prices are samples, and no card is ever charged. Real bookings and payments come next.
  • The dashboard saves on your device for now. Once it’s live, it sits behind a code sent to your email and saves to your Airtable base.
  • Where a season has no real photo yet, the picture says Imagined.
  • The films use five of the 91 drone clips so far; the rest are on their way.
  • The games on the place pages are short versions. The full ones are played at each place, on a phone, and some use its microphone, compass, motion or camera.
  • The sounds are made by the site itself for now. Thirty seconds recorded at each place, by you, would replace them.
