Imagined season
How it began

Cody and Nicole

They built the dome by hand, opened it in 2023, and welcomed the first 223 guests who wrote about their stay before handing it on.

Before the dome there was a hillside in Delaware County and an idea: a white sphere looking out at the ridge. Cody and Nicole raised it by hand, strut by strut, hub by hub — no single piece of it curved, only the whole. They opened it to guests in 2023, wrote its first guidebook, and ran it well enough, long enough, to earn Superhost status before they handed it on.
